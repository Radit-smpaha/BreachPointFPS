OPERATION BRIEF

OBJECTIVE — BREACH

Survive three waves of hostile operators to unlock C4 defusal. When a charge is armed, move within range and press F to defuse it — hold your position until the countdown finishes. Between waves, press B to buy weapons, armor and utility. Hostiles reinforce, flank and suppress — watch your compass and reload behind cover.

ENEMY BEHAVIOUR

  • Squad pathwaying — squads split into fire and flank elements, and share sighting information.
  • Cover search — enemies path to cover that blocks your line of sight, not just the closest box.
  • Suppression — sustained fire pins them; they blind-fire and call for support rather than peeking.
  • Leaning & peeking — they lean from cover to shoot, then tuck back to reload.
  • Utility — wounded or blocked enemies throw fragmentation and smoke grenades.
  • Last seen memory — they investigate your last known position, clear corners and hold angles.

FIELD TIPS

Reload behind cover — the enemy pushes the moment they see an empty mag. Headshots drop most hostiles instantly. Slide (Shift+Ctrl) to break their aim.
